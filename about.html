About and method

Part sound studio. Part science lab.

Hush Focus Music makes original sound for sleep, focus and calm, and looks carefully at what research says about the sounds people rest and work to. This page explains how we do both.

How we make the sound

  • Every sound on the channel is our own, built from scratch. We do not use stock loops, sample packs or other people's recordings.
  • Noise, tones, drones, rain and instruments are synthesised and shaped by us, then levelled to a consistent, comfortable loudness so nothing jumps out.
  • Our extended listening sessions have no vocals and no sudden interruptions.
  • When a title names a frequency or a tuning, such as 432 Hz, that is a description of how the sound was made. It is not a promise about what it will do for you.

How we handle research

  1. We read the papers, not the headlines. Each claim in an episode is tied to one named source, and every source is linked.
  2. Every study is shown with its size and its limits. Who took part, how many, for how long, and what the study cannot tell us.
  3. We use the researchers' own grading where it exists. If a systematic review rates its evidence as moderate or very low certainty, we report that rating as given. Where no formal grade exists, we say so instead of inventing one.
  4. We keep feelings and measurements apart. "People felt they slept better" and "a sleep recording showed a change" are different findings, and we label which is which.
  5. We say when the evidence runs out. Popular does not mean proven. If the research on a sound is thin, the episode says so.

What we do not do

  • We do not make medical claims or promise outcomes. Nothing here is medical advice.
  • We do not describe a single small study as settled science.
  • We do not publish a number we have not traced to its source.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, we fix it in the open. Corrections are recorded on the episode's research page and in the pinned comment on the video, with the date. To report an error, leave a comment on the relevant video.

How the episodes are produced

The Science of Sound is written, scored and animated in-house. The sound demonstrations are generated by us so that what you hear matches what is described. Episodes are narrated with a synthetic voice.
