Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Who we are

Hush Focus Music publishes focus and sleep sounds on YouTube and runs this website. Contact: through the About section of our YouTube channel.

This website

This site has no accounts, no forms and no analytics. We do not collect, store or sell personal information from visitors. The focus timer runs entirely in your browser.

The site embeds videos from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode. When you play a video, YouTube (Google) may collect data under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

The Hush Factory app

Hush Factory is a private tool used only by the Hush Focus Music team to upload and schedule videos on our own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

It uses YouTube API Services and asks for permission to manage our own YouTube account (scopes: youtube and youtube.upload). With that permission it uploads our videos, sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists, and reads our own channel's video list. It does not access any other person's Google or YouTube data.

The sign-in token is stored only on the device that runs the tool. It is not shared with or transferred to any third party. Text for titles and descriptions is drafted with an AI writing service; no Google user data is sent to it.

By using a product that relies on YouTube API Services, users agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and stored tokens are deleted on request or when access is revoked.
